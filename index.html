How long does a website take?

Most small business sites take about 7 days once I have your content. Bigger sites and online stores take longer, and I’ll give you a clear date before we start.

How does payment work?

50% to start the project and 50% when you’re happy and ready to launch. I quote a fixed price after the free mockup, so there are no surprises.

Can I edit the website myself?

Yes. WordPress lets you change text, photos and products without coding, and I’ll show you how at handover.

Do I need to buy hosting and a domain?

Yes, a domain and hosting are separate yearly costs paid to the provider. I can recommend good, affordable options in Pakistan and set everything up for you.

Do you fix existing websites?

Yes. Speed problems, broken layouts, errors and security issues are all common jobs, and I can often fix them within a few days.
